Welcome to Alexa Food Log

An intelligent Alexa skill that helps you track your daily nutrition through simple voice commands. Log your meals, track calories, protein, carbs, and fat, and stay on top of your health goals.

🎯 Easy Logging

Simply say "I ate two eggs and a banana" and Alexa will log it automatically.

📊 Smart Tracking

Get real-time nutrition totals and see how much you have left to reach your daily goals.

🔒 Privacy First

Your data is stored securely and never shared with third parties.

Privacy Policy: View Privacy Policy

How to Use

Enable the Alexa Food Log skill in your Alexa app, then start logging:

  • "Alexa, open Food Logger"
  • "I ate two eggs and a banana"
  • "What's my nutrition status?"
